

^8Alone at War - LEVEL 5^8

^8Classified: Top Secret^8
^8Codename: Camp Attack^8

Now that you have arrived at the Partisan camp, 
you must get your new orders from the head Partisan. 
Stay on alert, because the Nazis may launch an 
attack on the camp at any time. 
Be prepared for anything.

Also, be on the lookout for treasure that the 
Nazis may be hoarding.

Destroy the Nazi's radio communications, in 
order to cripple their ability to communicate. 
When you are finished, find a transport and escape.

Good luck, Soldier.
