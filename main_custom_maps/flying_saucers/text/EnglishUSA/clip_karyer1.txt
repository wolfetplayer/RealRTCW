To: Mary
From: Carl

My Dear Mary, I think about You every second. I am all right, I have not been taken prisoner and have not perished yet, as it is said, the "kindhearted neighbour". Hopefully, with our new weapon we soon can repulse the enemy. Feeding us has become worse. My friends were starved. The Commander gives hard work all the time. But I do not despair, because I have You. Hopefully, we will meet soon.

Forever yours, Carl
