                        Vril I

  Technical Data:

Diameter: 11,5 m. 
Drive: Schumann Levitator.
Control: Mag Field Impulser 3a.
Speed: 2900 km/h (theoretically up to 12000 km/h).
Flight endurance: 0,5 h.
Armament: 4x110 mm KSK in rotating turret and 2 x MK 108 8x50 mm KSK.
Armor: Double Victalen.
Crew: 1 (with room for up to 3 people)
Employment fitness: 100%
Hovering capability: 12 minutes.
All weather, day and night, capability.
First flight: 1943.
Available for service: 1944.
