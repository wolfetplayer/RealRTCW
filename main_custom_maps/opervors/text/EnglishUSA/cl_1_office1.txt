To: Major Heinz Soberhammer, Plateau Officer in Charge 
From: Oberst Bertrand B. Vorsichtig
Subject: Maintenance Duties

Dear Major, 

it has come to my attention that general maintenance on the Nazi Plateau has not been up to touch lately. I would strongly advise you to get the men of the Reich to do their duties with swiftness and punctuality.

In 4 days I will in person come down to the Plateau and inspect the conditions for myself.

In another matter, the Nazi Union Conference is being held at the Castle this week. Major F. Fleicher is welcoming many of our Officer friends from Berlin, and I would urge you to keep the level of security at the highest state of alert.

Outpost Commander in Chief,
B. B. Vorsichtig, Oberst
Reich Special Projects
