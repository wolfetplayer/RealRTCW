INCOMING RADIO MESSAGES, 
OUTPOST MOUNTAIN BASE

March 4, 1944:

Received messages from several guards about disturbances reported on the Plateau. Several soldiers apparently killed.

Reports of an american intruder. These messages have not been confirmed, as no further signals were received from the Radio Operators on the Plateau.

This should be investigated immediately.

Radio Operator #2,
Harry Michelstaz,
Lieutenant
