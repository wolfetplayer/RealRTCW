To: Major Fritz Fleicher, Kommandant, Outpost Castle
From: Oberst Bertrand B. Vorsichtig
Subject: Outpost Conference 

Dear Major, 

I want to thank you in writing for your efforts in preparing the Nazi Union conference at the Outpost Castle. 

I have given orders for all the Castle personnel to be on guard during the proceedings. Our intelligence have reported allied activity in the vicinity. It is my hope that this time we will be spared the grief of what happened last year at Castle Wolfenstein. 

If anyone at the Castle, or anywhere else on the Outpost, spots the american agent who was responsible for that failure I am to be notified immediately.

Outpost Commander in Chief,
B. B. Vorsichtig, Oberst
Reich Special Projects
