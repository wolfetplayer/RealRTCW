To: Miss Helga Munich, Secretary
From: Major Fritz Fleicher
Subject: Conference

Miss Helga,

this is a reminder about our visits from all parts of the Reich this week. Higher Officers and members of the Nazi Party will arrive this morning, and stay at the Castle all week. 

I urge you therefore to make sure the Castle maintenance team are up to their duties, and that clean bed-linens and other facilities are in order for their care and enjoyment.

I also wish to thank you for our time together last night. I know I should not be speaking about it in such notes, but I can't help myself, miss Helga! I hope we can meet again soon outside the boundaries of this Office. 

Kommandant
Fritz Fleicher, Major
Outpost Castle
