To: Work Personnel, Outpost Castle
From: Miss Helga Munich, Kommandantur Office
Subject: Castle Maintenance

NAZI UNION CONFERENCE PREPARATIONS 

Kommandant, Major F. Fleicher, has given orders that all radios and technical equipment should be in working order for the visiting Officers at the Castle this week.

This is a reminder to make sure none of our important guests will depart from the Castle with any complaints at the end of their stay.

A copy of this note has been sent to the Conference Room office, to prechamber secretary Eva Decker.

Miss Helga Munich, secretary
Kommandantur Office Prechamber
Outpost Castle
