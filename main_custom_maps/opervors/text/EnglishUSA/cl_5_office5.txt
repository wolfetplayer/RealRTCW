To: Work Personnel, Outpost Castle
From: Miss Helga Munich, Kommandantur Office
Subject: Gold Treasure Exhibition

Kommandant, Major F. Fleicher, has given orders to prepare a special exhibition for the visiting officers to show them our Gold Treasure in the Castle basement during the week.

His orders are that two soldiers from the Watch Team are to stand guard by the Gold Treasure at any time. They are to help out the officer in charge for the exhibition when that time comes. More details on this event will follow at a later stage of the Conference proceedings.

A copy of this note has been sent to the Conference Room office, to prechamber secretary Eva Decker.

Miss Helga Munich, secretary
Kommandantur Office Prechamber
Outpost Castle
