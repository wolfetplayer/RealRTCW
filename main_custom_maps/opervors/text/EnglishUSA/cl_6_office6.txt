To: Eva Decker, Conference Room office
From: Major Fritz Fleicher, Kommandant
Subject: Nazi Union Conference Notes

Eva,

I have given Helga orders about the preparations for the Nazi Union conference this week, and I have told her to inform you of the same duties.

During the proceedings, I will need you attending the Conference Table by my side. You need to carefully take notes as our guests are speaking their mind. It is very important that notes are being taken in detail. Afterwards the documents should be archived here at the Castle. 

A copy should be sent to Berlin immediately by the closing of the meeting at the end of the week.

Kommandant
Fritz Fleicher, Major
Outpost Castle
