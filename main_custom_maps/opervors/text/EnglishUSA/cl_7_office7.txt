Conference Proceedings Notes
Outpost Castle, Germany

Date: March 4, 1944

Meetings have not gone well. Major Klunchenstaz and Oberstlieutenant Weberplatz have objected against the plans that my Commanding Officer, Oberst B. B. Vorsichtig, told me to propose. Captain Schnietze and Major Gruzschtaler also had serious objections. 

After a while, I ordered a recess until tomorrow, so I can contact Oberst Vorsichtig about the rising matter.

I told them about Castle Wolfenstein, and that the rumoured Übersoldat does exist. They refused to believe the very idea of such a soldier. I must continue my persuading tomorrow, or else I fear Oberst Vorsichtig will not be merciful if I fail him again.

Fritz Fleicher, Major
Kommandant, Outpost Castle
