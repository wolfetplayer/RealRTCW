
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Special Forces^8

^8Part 3: Communication Center^8

According to our intel, there should be a 
communication center in the base.
You must eliminate two German officers that are
responsible for maintaining the
communication network in the 
Trondheim region.

After that, you must find and open the exit 
gate, which will lead you to the nearby 
forest. There, you should join up with the 
second squad of the Royal Marines.
