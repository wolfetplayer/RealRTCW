
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Special Forces^8

^8Part 6: Old Monastery^8

It seems the monastery is occupied by elite
German paratroopers, which were sent here
as reinforcements. They are equipped with the
newest German rifle FG-42, developed
specifically for paratroopers.

You must find a secret passage leading
to the old tunnel system. This is our key
to the Trondheim radar.

The secret passage can be opened with a lever,
which is located somewhere in the officers
quarters.

Also, you must eliminate three German
security officers, to prevent the Germans from
following us through the tunnels.
