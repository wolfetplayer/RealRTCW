
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Special Forces^8

^8Part 1: Beach Landing^8

For more than a month, the Germans have been 
holding the Norwegian port of Trondheim.
Trondheim is a key location in this area.
The one who controls Trondheim 
controls the whole region.

The Royal Navy is trapped in the North Sea,
because of the German coastal artillery.

The Royal Air Force cant bomb the
German fortifications, because of the new
German AA radar, which can spot our
planes easily.

We need to destroy this radar at any cost.
No support will be provided. You will 
land on the beach near the Trondheim radar.
After landing, it will be up to you to determine
the best possible course of action.
