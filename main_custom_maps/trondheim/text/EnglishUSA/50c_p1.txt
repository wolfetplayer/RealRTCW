
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Red Alert^8

^8Part 4: Mountain Pass^8

The mountain pass is the most difficult and
dangerous area. Its perfect firing range
for enemy snipers. However, this is your
only way to get to the West Trondheim base.
Use the tram to get to the other side.

Since you made a mess in the 
underground bunker, the Germans are 
probably going to be waiting for you.
