
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Red Alert^8

^8Part 5: Trondheim Base^8

You have reached the West Trondheim base!

Most of the German soldiers went to the
Trondheim radar to fight the British.

The encryption codes should be in a safe
somewhere in the base.
However, you can’t just open it.

There is no time to search for safe codes,
so you need to find a way to blow it up!

Also, you must eliminate the security officer
to prevent the Germans from calling in
additional reinforcements.
