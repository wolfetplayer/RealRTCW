
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Iron Cross^8

^8Part 1: Beach Attack^8

An unexpected attack from the British Marines
took us by surprise. Most of our soldiers
went to the West base to escort the recently 
arrived SS officers.

The British are aiming to destroy the Trondheim radar.
This will allow their bombers to strike on
our coastal fortifications.

However, the British attack is very
unorganized. It should be simple enough
to counterattack.

If possible, try to find any intel, which
could help us to predict their next move.
