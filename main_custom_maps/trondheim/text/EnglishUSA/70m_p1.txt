
^8Mission: Operation Trondheim - Iron Cross^8

^8Part 2: Forest Ambush^8

We successfully repelled the British attack,
but our West base is not responding
to our radio messages.

It could be technical difficulties.

The worst case scenario would be that the British captured our base and
sabotaged our communications.

We were able to call in reinforcements, but 
until they arrive, we should investigate
the situation ourselves.
